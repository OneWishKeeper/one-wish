The Lesser Key of King Solomon

The Lemegeton is a historical grimoire tradition divided into five books: Goetia, Theurgia-Goetia, Ars Paulina, Ars Almadel, and Ars Notoria. The best-known section is the Goetia, a catalogue of seventy-two named figures, ranks, seals, and attributed qualities.

On this site, the material is presented as historical literature, folklore, symbolism, and visual inspiration.

1. Bael

Traditionally listed as a king associated with hiddenness and command.

2. Agares

Traditionally described as a duke associated with languages and movement.

3. Vassago

Traditionally listed as a prince associated with finding what is concealed.

4. Samigina

Traditionally associated with learning, memory, and the liberal sciences.

5. Marbas

Traditionally associated with hidden causes, craft, and transformation.

6. Valefor

Traditionally listed as a duke associated with loyalty and temptation.
