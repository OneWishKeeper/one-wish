Preface

This page presents the First Book of the Lemegeton as a historical and literary archive. The well-known printed edition was assembled from older manuscript traditions and includes ceremonial descriptions, seals, classifications, and accounts of seventy-two named spirits.

Preliminary Invocation

“Thee I invoke, the Bornless One. Thee that didst create the Earth and the Heavens; the Night and the Day; the Darkness and the Light.”

The original text continues through a long series of sacred names and poetic declarations. On this webpage those passages are organized in expandable archive sections so visitors can read without being overwhelmed by one enormous wall of text.

The Initiated Interpretation

One influential interpretation in the printed edition treats the spirits and seals symbolically—as images connected with attention, imagination, memory, emotion, and different faculties of the human mind.

Five Books of the Lemegeton

The traditional work is divided into Goetia, Theurgia-Goetia, Ars Paulina, Ars Almadel, and Ars Notoria. This page focuses on the Goetia and its seventy-two catalogued figures.
