The Lesser Key of King Solomon

The Lemegeton is a historical grimoire tradition divided into five books: Goetia, Theurgia-Goetia, Ars Paulina, Ars Almadel, and Ars Notoria. The best-known section is the Goetia, a catalogue of seventy-two named figures, ranks, seals, appearances, and attributed offices.

This webpage treats those claims as historical literature, folklore, symbolism, and visual inspiration. The cards below open into individual entries rather than leading away from the page.

Ranks and Titles
